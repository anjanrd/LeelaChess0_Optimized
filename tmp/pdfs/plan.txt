Compression  ablation  
The  main  ladder  is  cumulative.  Each  step  keeps  everything  before  it  and  adds  one  technique.  
--- PAGE 8 ---
Step  Model  What  it  adds  
0  FP16  Baseline  
1  Naive  W8A8  PTQ  Per-tensor  weights,  max  calibration,  random  positions  
2  +  per-channel  weights  A  separate  scale  for  each  output  channel  
3  +  better  calibration  Entropy  or  percentile  instead  of  max  
4  +  SmoothQuant  Moves  activation  outliers  into  the  weights,  with  the  scales  folded  into  the  preceding  layernorm  
5  +  search-aware  calibration  
Calibration  on  positions  the  search  actually  visits  
6  +  QAT  Fine-tuning  with  fake  quantization  (Should)  
7  +  KD  Distillation  from  FP16  during  QAT  (Should)
